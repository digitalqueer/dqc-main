Politically, 2025 was a new experience for the United States. The results of the current administration's actions are certainly in line with the nation's history; however, the pace of executive action is unprecedented, dangerous, and outright irresponsible. The descent into an authoritative state has started, and queer rights are already being used as a playground for institutions that have elected to contribute to or comply with a societal redefinition process.

There is an obvious need for queer advocacy, dissent, and collective action against the efforts of this current movement.

The most primitive goal of DQC(3) is to start its public efforts. 

Ideas for DQC(3) came as a response to the anti-queer executive orders signed into law by the Trump administration and the plethora of anti-queer ideas in the administration's unofficial, but  obvious blueprint, Project 2025. While the founders were daydreaming about forming DQC(3), they were actively involved in assisting DQC(3)'s mother organization, [Trans Formations Project (TFP)](https://transformationsproject.net). Then TFP closed its doors. They ceased its operations after the assassination of Charlie Kirk. The anti-trans narrative attached itself to this pivotal event within hours of event's initial reports. In response to this narrative and for the safety of the organization's members, TFP announced its plans to completely close the organization 6 days after the event. 

The temperature was rising.

## Looking Forward

2026 is a pivotal year. Politically, it's a midterm year and marks 1 year of the second Trump administration's controversial control. Socially, general civil unrest is growing, and many of the current administration's pet projects are facing public backlash. For new and old organizations alike, this is a promising year where we can be heard. DQC(3) plans to support a hopefully, reinvigorated Queer Liberation movement and bring queer ethics into the conversations that cis heteronormative *malfeasants* are trying to dominate. 

As a new organization, DQC(3) wants input from the community. We believe that using our voice as a collective to bring awareness to our shared issues is the best use of our time and resources. Queerness is inherently a celebration of diversity, and while DQC(3)'s founders live within the community, they recognize that acting as if their perspective is true for all members serves no one. **We want to hear from you.** We want to hear your concerns, observations, and where you feel like systems are failing you. Starting in Spring 2026, we will be rolling out a series of community surveys and virtual discussions to gather these opinions in a way that respects your privacy and safety above all else.

In the meantime, we would like to continue the work that pulled us into the nonprofit advocacy world initially. TFP diligently focused on tracking the daunting amount of anti-trans legislation popping up across state legislatures from 2021 until its closing in 2025. For 2026, storing TFP's archive of bill data from past years, supporting other aligned organizations such as [Trans Legislation](https://translegislation.com/), and carrying the torch forward is a collective priority. 

### The Impact Fellowship

One of the first programs designed for DQC(3) was the *Impact Fellowship*. A program where a talented member of the collective can provide their services to a partner organization within a multi-week long residency. During the residency, the member would dedicate significant hours to leveraging their technical expertise in web development, software engineering, and digital innovation to support a nonprofit organization that is advancing queer justice and/or intersecting social causes in some way, shape, form, or capacity. All while DQC(3) provides a stipend and enables the member to provide at a capacity greater than typical volunteer work. Ideally, DQC(3) hopes to start exploring partner organizations this summer, and find a well-aligned placement by winter. 

## Wrapping up

This post is being published three weeks into 2026, and this year is already shaping up to be another difficult year for the rights and safety of many marginalized communities in the United States, 2SLGBTQIA+ people included. As an new, small organization, DQC(3) cannot solve or mitigate the effects of our government's slide into authoritarianism; however, we are determined to start building and supporting an *Infrastructure of Change* for the queer community and individuals. We hope that we can use our privilege, talents, and  time to lighten this societal burden on our beloved siblings.
